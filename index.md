# Privacy Policy — Void Hearth

**Effective date:** 2026-05-17  
**Developer:** Cullusion  
**App:** Void Hearth (Android)  
**Contact:** [support@dxmcompliance.com](mailto:support@dxmcompliance.com)

## 1. Overview

Void Hearth is an idle incremental game. Your game progress is saved locally on your device. We do not require account creation, and we do not sell your personal data to third parties.

This policy describes the data collected by third-party SDKs integrated into the app and how that data is used.

---

## 2. Data We Collect

Void Hearth itself does not directly collect, store, or transmit personal information. However, the following third-party services are integrated and may collect data as described below.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

### Google AdMob
Void Hearth displays optional rewarded advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

- **Data collected:** Advertising ID, device identifiers, IP address, approximate location (derived from IP), ad interaction data.
- **Purpose:** To serve relevant ads and measure ad performance.
- **Opt-out:** You can opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings under **Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization**.
- **Privacy policy:** [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### RevenueCat
In-app purchases (the Eternal Warden Pack) are managed by RevenueCat.

- **Data collected:** Purchase receipt data, a randomly generated user ID, device type, and purchase timestamps. RevenueCat does not collect payment card numbers (those are handled entirely by Google Play).
- **Purpose:** To verify and restore purchases.
- **Privacy policy:** [https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)

### Firebase Analytics (Google)
We use Firebase Analytics to understand how players engage with the game.

- **Data collected:** Anonymous usage events (e.g., session length, feature usage, Collapse counts), device model, OS version, and country.
- **Purpose:** To improve game balance and player experience. Data is aggregated and not linked to your identity.
- **Privacy policy:** [https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

### Firebase Crashlytics (Google)
We use Firebase Crashlytics to detect and fix crashes.

- **Data collected:** Crash logs, stack traces, device state at the time of crash, device model, OS version.
- **Purpose:** To identify and fix bugs that affect stability.
- **Privacy policy:** [https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

### Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google)
We use Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver optional push notifications.

- **Data collected:** A Firebase registration token linked to your device. This token is not linked to your identity.
- **Purpose:** To send optional game notifications (e.g., hearth activity reminders). You can disable notifications at any time in your Android device settings.
- **Privacy policy:** [https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

---

## 4. Data Storage

All game save data is stored locally on your device using Android's local storage. No save data is transmitted to our servers.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

Void Hearth is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data through this app, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

---

## 6. Data Retention

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Data retained by third-party services is subject to their respective retention policies (see links above).
